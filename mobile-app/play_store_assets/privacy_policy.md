# Privacy Policy for MyVault

**Last updated: September 19, 2026**

MyVault ("we", "our", or "us") operates the MyVault mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you:

- **Personal Data**: When registering, you may provide your Name, College/University Email address, Student Roll Number/USN, Engineering Branch, and Current Semester.
- **Authentication Data**: Managed securely through **Google Firebase Authentication** (JWT tokens, OAuth 2.0).
- **Academic Documents**: Files and notes stored in **Amazon Web Services (AWS S3)** with AES-256 server-side encryption.
- **Usage Data**: We may collect non-identifiable usage statistics, quiz participation scores, and app crash telemetry.

## 2. Use of Data

MyVault uses the collected data for various purposes:
- To provide and maintain the Service (e.g., displaying study materials matching your branch and semester).
- To notify you about academic announcements and placement drives.
- To provide in-app customer support and process feedback.
- To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and crash reporting.

## 3. Data Security & Storage

The security of your data is important to us. All student records, certificates, and authentication tokens are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 encryption.

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure:
- **Google Firebase**: Authentication, Cloud Messaging & Crash Telemetry.
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**: Permanent S3 Cloud Storage.
- **MongoDB & PostgreSQL**: Distributed database clustering.
